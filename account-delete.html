Request account & data deletion

Updated 1 July 2569 (2026) · English edition of the Thai text above

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the IUFIT app
  2. Go to ⚙️ Settings → Privacy & consent
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Deleting removes, immediately: your account, your cloud backup, and your user registration

Option 2 — send us a request

If you cannot get into the app, or you would rather we did it for you, just send us a request. Tell us the email address or LINE account you signed up with, so we can verify who you are.

Email a deletion request Message us on LINE @987qyznd

We will action it within 30 days, as required by the PDPA, and confirm back to you once it is done

What will be deleted

  • Profile data (name, sex, date of birth, height, weight, photo)
  • Health / exercise data (weight, body measurements, food logs, training, water intake)
  • Cloud backup (Firebase) and your user registration
  • The email address / LINE account linked to your account

Photos / videos you have sent in chat (stored on Cloudflare R2) are already auto-deleted within 24 hours

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone — if you want to keep your data, export it (Backup/Export) in Settings before you delete
