Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Last updated July 2026 · Aligned with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

English translation of the Thai original (privacy.html), version 1.2. The documents do not designate a prevailing language version.

IUFIT ("we") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal data, including your rights as a data subject.

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

IUFIT — a health and fitness application provided over the web/PWA and through LINE
Personal data enquiries: email support@iufit.com · LINE Official Account (@987qyznd)

2. Data we collect

  • Identity / contact data: email, name and (for coaches) phone number and province
  • Health data sensitive data: weight, body measurements, food logs and exercise
  • Usage data: sign-ins, features used, signup channel, device id and the time each entry was recorded
  • Payment data: the package subscribed to, billing cycle, payment status and payment reference · we do not store credit/debit card numbers (see section 4)
  • Technical data as far as necessary to provide the service

3. Purposes for which we use your data

PurposeLegal basis
Providing the app (recording, analysing and displaying health data)Consent (health data) / performance of a contract
Giving the coach you choose access to the relevant health dataYour consent
Analysing and improving the app (using usage data, not health data)Consent
Taking payment for subscription packages, issuing proof of payment and activating your entitlementPerformance of a contract
Contacting you and sending news / promotionsConsent

You can consent to each purpose separately and withdraw your consent at any time under Settings → Privacy & consent (withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal)

4. Disclosure / sharing of data

  • Your coach — only the data you consent to give them access to
  • Service providers (data processors): Google Firebase (storage / authentication), Cloudflare (back end), Resend (email delivery), Omise (credit/debit card payment processing) — used only as far as necessary to provide the service
  • Your card details: when you pay through the website, your card details (card number, expiry date, CVV) are encrypted and sent directly from your browser to Omise, our payment service provider · IUFIT does not store or record your card number. Our systems receive only a payment reference (token) together with the package / billing cycle / user account, in order to activate your entitlement · Omise's use of data is governed by Omise's own privacy policy
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use health data for advertising

5. Transfers of data abroad

Some data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the Singapore region (asia-southeast1), which has appropriate data protection measures in place

6. Retention Period

We keep your data for as long as your account is in use, and will delete it or make it non-identifiable when you delete your account, or when it is no longer necessary for the purpose (statistical usage data may be kept in non-identifiable form)

7. Your rights under the PDPA

  • The right of access and to request a copy of your data (an Export function is available in the app)
  • The right to have your data corrected
  • The right to erase your data / delete your account (in Settings)
  • The right to withdraw consent
  • The right to object to / suspend the use of your data
  • The right to data portability

8. Security

Health data is stored primarily on your own device, and synced to encrypted cloud storage only as far as necessary, with access restricted by security rules (the data subject and system administrators only)

9. Data of minors

This service is suitable for users who have reached the age of majority. A minor should obtain the consent of a parent or guardian as required by law

10. Exercising your rights, and complaints

To exercise your rights or to ask a question, contact support@iufit.com or our LINE Official Account (@987qyznd) · If you consider that we are not complying with the law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC)

11. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all your data yourself in the app (Settings → Privacy → Delete account), or see how and send us a request on the Request account & data deletion page

12. Health data from Health Connect (Android app)

For users of the IUFIT app on Android, we may connect to Health Connect in order to read your activity data. You must always grant permission first:

  • Data types we read (read-only, never written back): Steps, Distance, Calories burned, Heart rate, Sleep and Exercise sessions
  • Purpose: to display your activity inside the app only (for example daily step / calorie / sleep summaries) and to combine it with your in-app records so you can see an overall picture of your health
  • Storage: data from Health Connect is kept on your device and is not uploaded to our servers automatically
  • Sharing: we do not sell it, do not share it with third parties and do not use it for advertising · it is not sent to your coach unless you give explicit consent, and it is not sent to any AI system
  • Your control: you can grant or revoke permission for each data type at any time in the Health Connect app · you can disconnect in the IUFIT app (data already recorded remains) · and you can delete the data

IUFIT's use and transfer of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We may update this policy from time to time, and will state / display the date of the most recent update at the top
