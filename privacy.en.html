Privacy Policy

Version 1.5 · Effective 7 October 2026 · In line with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA)

IUFIT ("we") is an app that helps fitness coaches look after their trainees. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect your personal data, and what rights you have. It applies to IUFIT on the web/PWA, the Android app, the iOS (iPhone) app and the IUFIT LINE Official Account.

1. Who we are (data controller)

IUFIT is operated by Weerapong Sae-ieo (an individual), 9 Ramkhamhaeng 170, Min Buri, Bangkok 10510, who is the controller of personal data collected through the app · "We" in this policy means this operator · Privacy contact: support@iufit.com · LINE Official Account (@987qyznd)

The coach you choose to connect with looks after you directly and uses your data for that purpose as described in section 4.

2. Data we collect

  • Account and contact: email (sign-in with an emailed code) · name or nickname · profile photo (if added) · for coaches: display name, storefront details and payout details the coach enters (such as a PromptPay ID)
  • Health data Sensitive: weight, body fat percentage, muscle mass, body measurements (neck, chest, waist, hips, upper arm, thigh, calf), goals, workout logs, food logs, sleep hours, and cautions or pain areas you report
  • Photos and clips: food photos you send, profile photos, homework photos or clips attached in chat, and exercise clips you send for your coach to check your form (form check · up to 60 seconds each · clips recorded with the in-app camera have no sound · clips picked from your gallery are sent as they are), together with the training day, the moves, any note you add and your coach's review (text and points marked on the clip)
  • Messages: chat messages and stickers with your coach · messages you type to the IU Mate assistant
  • Purchase data (coaches): plan, billing cycle, amount, status and reference IDs from Google Play or Omise · credit balance and credit history · we never store card numbers
  • Device and usage data: app device ID, log times, language, operating system, and aggregated feature usage (only when you turn on "Usage data")
  • App error reports (automatic): when the app hits an error we receive the error message, the screen where it happened (without link parameters), the app version and the platform (web/Android/iOS) · no account ID, email or device ID, and Thai text, emails, phone numbers, numbers and data values are removed before sending, so no health data is included
  • IU Mate chat reports (only when you tap): when you tap "Report chat" we send only the question and answer shown on screen (weight, height, age, calorie target and BMI numbers are removed before sending) so our team can improve answers

4. Who sees your data

  • Your coach — when you connect with a coach's code or link and consent at sign-up, that coach sees your workout logs, food logs (including food photos), weight and measurements, sleep hours, cautions you report, and your chat, in order to look after you · Coaches only see their own trainees · When you stop following a coach, that coach can no longer see your data in the app (messages and notes the coach wrote earlier stay with the coach) · Your coach can log measurements for you (for example at the gym); entries made by your coach are labelled as such in your history · Once you stop following a coach, they can no longer see or log measurements for you
  • Cards you share yourself — a result card you choose to share shows only what is on the card, and you choose whether to show your coach's name
  • Coach share requests — your coach can share your result card (for example on the coach's own channels) only after you approve that request · You choose how your name appears · No answer or a decline means it cannot be shared · You can withdraw approval at any time on the privacy screen (IUFIT cannot remove a card the coach already posted elsewhere before you withdrew)
  • Service providers (processors acting for us) — used only as needed to run the service and not allowed to use your data for other purposes:
    • Google Firebase — authentication and database (Singapore region) · notifications in the Android app (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
    • Apple — notifications in the iPhone app through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs)
    • Web notifications — your browser's notification service (Web Push) · notifications on every channel never contain health numbers, but notifications to a coach may include the trainee's name and a pain area the trainee reported
    • Cloudflare — backend and photo storage
    • Google Gemini — nutrition estimates from food photos (only that photo is sent; no name, email or account ID) · the IU Mate assistant (your message, recent conversation and basic facts such as sex, age, weight (only values you or your coach entered, never values imported from Health Connect or Apple Health), height, goal and activity level; no name, email, allergies or medical conditions) · program or meal-plan drafts when a coach taps (goal, level, equipment and caution areas; no name)
    • Google Play — in-app payments on Android
    • Omise — card and PromptPay payments on the web · card details go straight from your browser to Omise and IUFIT never stores card numbers
    • LINE — notifications and photo intake through the LINE Official Account (when you link LINE)
    • Resend — sign-in code, coach welcome and receipt emails
  • Coach PromptPay payouts — when a trainee pays a coach for a course by PromptPay, the money goes directly between trainee and coach; IUFIT does not receive or hold it
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use health data for advertising · We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it

5. International transfers

The database is hosted on Google Firebase in Singapore (asia-southeast1) · Photos and backend run on Cloudflare's network · AI processing runs on Google's systems · Transfers to these providers rely on the data processing terms of Google and Cloudflare, which set personal data protection safeguards, and for health data also on the explicit consent you give at sign-up.

6. Retention

  • Account data, health data, logs and chat: kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account (section 11)
  • Food photos: deleted automatically 60 days after sending · homework photos and clips in chat: deleted automatically within 24 hours
  • Form check clips: visible only to you and the coach you are connected with · viewable for 7 days after sending, then deleted automatically (the file is removed within 8 days of sending) · a coach you have disconnected from can no longer open them · reviews and training-day details stay with your logs until you delete your account · deleting your account removes any remaining clips immediately
  • System backups: kept for no more than 14 days, then deleted automatically
  • Purchase data and payment records: kept for at least 5 years as required by accounting and tax law, even after account deletion
  • App error reports: contain nothing that identifies you and are kept to track and fix app problems
  • IU Mate chat reports you sent: kept to improve answers even after account deletion (personal numbers removed before sending)
  • Usage statistics: kept aggregated or de-identified

7. Your rights under the PDPA

  • Right to access and obtain a copy (there is a data export button in the app)
  • Right to correct your data
  • Right to erase data or delete your account (section 11)
  • Right to withdraw consent (section 3)
  • Right to object or restrict processing
  • Right to data portability

8. Security

All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) · Database access rules allow reads only by the data owner, the coach you connect with, and necessary system administrators · Food photos open through signed links that expire within 10 minutes · Staff access is limited to what is necessary.

9. Minors

The service is designed for people aged 18 and over. Minors need parental consent as required by law · If we learn that we hold a minor's data without such consent, we will delete it.

10. Exercising your rights and complaints

Contact support@iufit.com, LINE Official Account (@987qyznd) or by post to Weerapong Sae-ieo, 9 Ramkhamhaeng 170, Min Buri, Bangkok 10510; we reply within 30 days · If you believe we have not complied with the law, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand.

11. Deleting your account and data

Delete your account in the app (trainees: Settings → Delete account · coaches: Me → Account → Delete account) or see how to request it on the account and data deletion page · Deletion removes your health data, logs, food photos, chat messages you sent, backups and sign-in account, and disconnects you from your coach · Anything left in system backups is gone within 14 days · Purchase data that the law requires us to keep is the exception (section 6).

12. Health data from Health Connect (Android) and Apple Health (iPhone)

The IUFIT Android app reads data from Health Connect only when you tap "Pull from Health Connect" on the weight log or body measurement screen, and you must always grant permission first:

  • Data types read (read-only, never written back): weight and body fat percentage
  • Purpose: fill your weight log with readings from your scale or other apps so you do not have to type them
  • Storage: the values are saved as weight log entries in the app, the same as entries you type yourself
  • Sharing: those weight entries are visible to the coach you connect with, as described in section 4 · never sold, never used for ads, never sent to AI
  • Your control: revoke each permission at any time in the Health Connect app · disconnect in the IUFIT app

IUFIT's use and transfer of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

On iPhone: when you tap "Import from Health", IUFIT reads only your weight and body-fat percentage from Apple Health (HealthKit). We never write to Apple Health, never use this data for advertising or marketing, never sell or share it with anyone other than the coach you connect with, and never store Apple Health data in iCloud. You can revoke access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › IUFIT.
